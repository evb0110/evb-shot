From a quick capture to a little more context

⌗

A region. A window. The screen.

Choose from the menu bar and capture at native resolution. Window captures come without added backgrounds or shadows.

↧

Long pages, original pixels.

Select a scrolling region and scroll downward with overlap. The stitched PNG retains its full resolution, even for very tall images.

↗

Make your point.

Add text, arrows, and rectangles. Crop, zoom, undo, and redo. Editable drafts keep your original image and annotations separate.

◉

Record with the controls in reach.

Pick native or downscaled resolution and 15, 24, 30, or 60 FPS right in the recording window.

▣

Your face and voice, when you want.

Include your microphone, computer audio, or a selfie camera in a corner. All three are off by default. Audio-only recording is available too.

↳

Save. Paste. Done.

Save instantly to your chosen folder and close the editor. Save & Copy Path also puts the full, safely quoted file path on the clipboard.

Small by design.

A native menu bar app, built with Swift and Apple’s capture frameworks. No browser runtime, cloud uploads, or global capture shortcuts.

Early, and useful

This is an evolving prototype. Scrolling capture is manual: exclude fixed headers and keep an overlap between views. Repeated or blank content can be difficult to align. Camera and microphone behavior may vary by device.
